KVKK Disclosure Notice

Last updated: 5 September 2026

Under Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), Serhat DAĞ is the data controller for Wageora to the extent the processing activities described here are subject to KVKK. Contact: serhatdagapps@gmail.com.

1. Data categories. Wageora processes profile information, job/pay settings, shifts and breaks, schedules, leave/PTO, goals, payslip fields and app settings locally on the device. Separate technical data may be processed by integrated services: Google Mobile Ads/UMP advertising and consent data; Google ML Kit device/app, per-installation, performance/API, event and error diagnostics; and app-store purchase/verification data such as product/store identifiers, transaction information, receipt/JWS verification material and an opaque refresh token.

2. Purposes, methods and legal conditions. Local work data is processed by automated means on the device to provide time/pay calculations, analysis, reminders, reports and user-requested backup/export functions. Limited SDK or HTTPS data flows are used for advertising/privacy choices, diagnostics, purchase validation, security and fraud prevention. Processing is carried out only where an applicable condition under KVKK Article 5 exists; depending on the activity this may include performance of the user-requested service/contract, compliance with legal obligations, legitimate interests that do not override fundamental rights, or explicit consent where the law requires consent.

3. Recipients and transfers. Core Wageora work history is not sent to a Wageora account/cloud server. Limited technical, advertising/consent and purchase-verification data may be disclosed to or processed by Google (Google Mobile Ads, UMP and ML Kit), Apple App Store or Google Play, and the infrastructure used for Wageora's HTTPS purchase-verification service. These providers may process data outside Türkiye. Any international transfer for which Wageora is responsible must use a lawful mechanism under applicable KVKK Article 9 requirements; provider-side processing is also subject to the provider's own privacy terms and legal obligations.

4. Retention, deletion and security. Wageora's active local work database is encrypted at rest with a device-protected 256-bit key. Portable `.wageora` backups are user initiated and use AES-256-GCM; the device storage key is not included in them. Reset Data removes active local work/profile/settings records but may preserve separate store entitlement, rewarded-pass anti-abuse timing, unfinished verification state and the device encryption key. Third-party and verifier data is retained only for the periods required by the relevant service, legal obligation, security/fraud-prevention need or valid entitlement lifecycle.

5. Rights under Article 11. You may contact serhatdagapps@gmail.com to exercise applicable rights under KVKK Article 11, including learning whether personal data is processed, requesting information, learning purposes and recipients, requesting correction where data is incomplete or inaccurate, requesting deletion/destruction where legal conditions are met, and exercising the other rights provided by law. Local Wageora work records can also be removed from within the app.

6. Minimization and consent controls. Wageora's purchase verifier is designed not to receive jobs, shifts, pay rules, payslip content, notes, reports or backups; raw receipt/JWS bodies are not intended for routine analytics. Advertising requests are gated by Google UMP's consent/privacy state where required, and privacy options can be revisited when Google requires an entry point. Wageora does not proactively request ATT in the current release. Material changes to these processing activities require the notice to be updated.
