KVKK Disclosure Notice

2026-08-31

Under Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), the data controller for Wageora is Serhat DAĞ. Contact: serhatdagapps@gmail.com.

1. Data categories. Profile details, job/pay settings, shift and break records, schedules, leave/PTO, goals and payslip fields entered by the user are processed locally on the device. If permitted, the app also manages notification preferences and Wageora Plus status.

2. Purposes and method. These data are processed by automated means on the device to provide app functions, time/pay calculations, analysis, reminders and user-requested backup/reporting actions. Core work history is not transferred to a Wageora-operated server. Processing necessary to provide the service relies on the applicable legal grounds under Article 5 of the KVKK; advertising/choice processing that requires consent is based on the user's choice where applicable.

3. Third parties and transfers. Wageora Free may use Google Mobile Ads. Google may process data such as IP address/general-location estimation, app interactions, diagnostics and device/advertising identifiers for ad delivery, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Google UMP consent/privacy-choice flows are used where required. On iOS, Plus payments are processed by Apple App Store. Apple-provided product/transaction/receipt verification data may be transferred over HTTPS to Wageora's purchase-verification service to validate Plus; local shift or payslip history is not sent for this verification.

4. Retention and deletion. Local work data is kept on the device and can be removed through the in-app data-deletion action. Purchased Plus can be restored through the user's Apple account; timing used to prevent abuse of rewarded Plus Passes may be kept separately. Data processed by Google and Apple is subject to their own retention policies.

5. Rights under Article 11. You may contact serhatdagapps@gmail.com to exercise applicable rights regarding whether personal data is processed, requesting information, learning the purpose and recipients of processing, requesting correction or deletion when legal conditions are met, and other rights provided by Article 11.

This notice describes Wageora's current data flows and will be updated if the app's data-processing structure changes.
