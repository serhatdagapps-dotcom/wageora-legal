Under Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), Serhat DAĞ is the data controller for Wageora to the extent the processing activities described here are subject to KVKK. Contact: serhatdagapps@gmail.com.

1. Data categories. Wageora processes profile information, job/pay settings, shifts and breaks, schedules, leave/PTO, goals, payslip fields and app settings locally on the device.

2. Purposes, methods and legal conditions. Local work data is processed by automated means on the device to provide time/pay calculations, analysis, reminders, reports and user-requested backup/export functions. Processing is carried out only where an applicable condition under KVKK Article 5 exists; depending on the activity this may include performance of the user-requested service/contract, compliance with legal obligations, legitimate interests that do not override fundamental rights, or explicit consent where the law requires consent.

3. Recipients and transfers. Core Wageora work history is not sent to a Wageora account/cloud server. These providers may process data outside Türkiye. Any international transfer for which Wageora is responsible must use a lawful mechanism under applicable KVKK Article 9 requirements; provider-side processing is also subject to the provider's own privacy terms and legal obligations.

4. Payments, StoreKit and international transfers. Wageora Plus payments are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play, and the developer does not access your full payment-card details. On iOS, paid Plus entitlement is checked on the device with Apple StoreKit 2; Wageora does not operate a separate purchase-verification backend and does not send purchase-verification material to a Wageora server. Google and Apple/Google Play may process limited technical, advertising/consent or purchase data outside Türkiye under their policies and applicable law. International transfers for which Wageora is responsible use a mechanism permitted by Article 9 of KVKK. You choose where exported PDF/CSV files and backups are saved or shared.

5. Rights under Article 11. You may contact serhatdagapps@gmail.com to exercise applicable rights under KVKK Article 11, including learning whether personal data is processed, requesting information, learning purposes and recipients, requesting correction where data is incomplete or inaccurate, requesting deletion/destruction where legal conditions are met, and exercising the other rights provided by law. Local Wageora work records can also be removed from within the app.

6. Minimization and consent controls. Advertising requests are gated by Google UMP's consent/privacy state where required, and privacy options can be revisited when Google requires an entry point. Wageora does not proactively request ATT in the current release. Material changes to these processing activities require the notice to be updated.
