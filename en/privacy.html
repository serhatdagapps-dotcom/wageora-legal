Privacy Policy

2026-08-31

Wageora is a local-first shift, hours and pay application operated by Serhat DAĞ. Contact: serhatdagapps@gmail.com.

1. Work data stored on your device. Profile details, jobs, pay rules, shifts, breaks, schedules, leave/PTO records, goals, payslip fields and app settings are stored locally on your device. Wageora does not operate an account/cloud service that uploads your work history to its own server.

2. Payslip OCR. On supported devices, text recognition for payslip images runs on-device. OCR results are added to local Wageora records only if you choose to save them.

3. Advertising and consent. Wageora Free may use Google Mobile Ads on eligible secondary screens outside Home and for optional rewarded videos. Google SDKs may process data such as IP address/general-location estimation, app interactions, diagnostics and device/advertising identifiers for ad delivery, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Where required, Wageora uses Google UMP consent/privacy-choice flows before requesting ads; choices can be reviewed again from Privacy Center.

4. Wageora Plus and purchase verification. On iOS, payment is processed by Apple App Store; Wageora does not receive your full payment-card details. To securely verify Plus entitlement, Apple-provided product/transaction/receipt verification data may be sent over HTTPS to Wageora's purchase-verification service. This verification does not include your shift history, payslip contents, notes or full payment-card details.

5. Notifications. If you grant permission, planned-shift, long-shift and optional break reminders may be scheduled on your device. Notification permission is controlled by the operating system and can be changed in settings.

6. Backup and export. PDF/CSV reports and password-protected Wageora backups are created only when you start the relevant action. You choose where files are saved or shared. Wageora does not store your backup password.

7. Retention and deletion. Local records may remain on the device until you delete them, uninstall the app, or complete the in-app data-deletion action. A purchased Plus entitlement can be restored through your Apple account; timing used to prevent abuse of rewarded Plus Passes may be kept separately from local work records. Data processed by Google and Apple is subject to those providers' retention policies.

8. Children. Wageora is not directed to children and does not knowingly request personal data from children.

9. Your choices and contact. For privacy, access, correction or deletion requests, contact serhatdagapps@gmail.com. From Privacy Center you can delete Wageora data stored on the device and, where available, review advertising privacy choices.
