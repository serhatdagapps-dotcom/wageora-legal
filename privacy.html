Wageora

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Wageora is a local-first shift, hours and pay application operated by Serhat DAĞ. Contact: serhatdagapps@gmail.com.

1. Local work data and storage security. Profile details, jobs, pay rules, shifts, breaks, schedules, leave/PTO records, goals, payslip fields and app settings are kept in Wageora's live local database. That database is encrypted at rest with a 256-bit device-protected key. Wageora does not operate an account or cloud-sync service that receives your work history. Live app storage is configured to be excluded from operating-system cloud backup or device transfer where supported.

2. Payslip OCR and ML Kit diagnostics. If you choose a payslip image on a supported mobile device, text recognition runs on-device and extracted text is added to Wageora records only if you choose to save it. Wageora does not intentionally upload the payslip image or recognized text to its own server. Google ML Kit may send Google technical diagnostics and usage metrics such as device/app information, per-installation identifiers, performance and API configuration, feature/event information and error codes. These metrics are separate from Wageora's local work-history database.

3. Advertising and consent. Wageora Free may show Google Mobile Ads adaptive banners on eligible Calendar and Pay surfaces and may offer an optional rewarded video for a temporary Plus Pass. Home has no banner, and Wageora does not use interstitial or app-open ads. Google Mobile Ads and UMP may process or share data such as IP address/general-location estimation, product interactions, diagnostics/performance information, advertising data and device/account identifiers for ad delivery, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Wageora refreshes UMP consent information on native launch; where a form is required, ad requests are gated by Google's `canRequestAds()` state, and privacy choices can be reopened from Privacy Center when Google requires an entry point.

4. Wageora Plus and store billing. Where paid Plus is offered, monthly and yearly subscriptions are processed by the platform app store, including Apple App Store or Google Play as applicable, and the store displays the local price, billing period and renewal terms. Wageora does not receive your full payment-card details. On iOS, Wageora checks paid Plus on the device using Apple StoreKit 2 verified current entitlements. Wageora does not operate a separate purchase-verification backend and does not send store purchase-verification material to a Wageora server. Only minimal local continuity state, such as product ID, entitlement expiry and last verified time, may be cached; this state is excluded from portable Wageora backups.

5. StoreKit and on-device entitlement state. On iOS, only entitlements verified by Apple StoreKit 2 determine paid Plus. Minimal entitlement state, rewarded-Plus anti-abuse timing and the device encryption key may remain locally separate from work history; deleting Wageora data does not itself cancel a store subscription.

6. Notifications. If you grant permission, planned-shift, long-shift and optional break reminders can be scheduled on your device. Notification permission is controlled by the operating system and can be changed in system settings. Wageora does not upload your shift history to a server to schedule these reminders.

7. Backup and export. PDF/CSV reports and password-protected `.wageora` backups are created only when you start the relevant action. Portable Wageora backups use AES-256-GCM with password-derived key material; Wageora does not store or recover your backup password, and the device storage-encryption key is not exported in the backup. You choose where exported files are saved or shared.

8. Retention and deletion. Work/profile/settings records remain in Wageora's active local storage until you remove them, reset Wageora data or the platform removes the app's local data. Reset Data removes those active local records but does not cancel an app-store subscription. Minimal StoreKit entitlement state, rewarded-Plus anti-abuse timing and the device encryption key may remain locally separate from work history and are excluded from portable Wageora backups where applicable.

9. Children. Wageora is not directed to children and does not knowingly ask children to provide personal work or payroll information.

10. Your choices, tracking strategy and contact. From Privacy Center you can remove Wageora's active local records and, where available, reopen advertising privacy choices. Camera/photo-library and notification permissions are controlled by the operating system. Wageora does not proactively request Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission and does not include an app-defined ATT prompt; Google Mobile Ads can use only identifiers made available by the operating system and applicable privacy/consent choices. For privacy, access, correction or deletion questions concerning Wageora-controlled data, contact serhatdagapps@gmail.com. Where the GDPR applies, Wageora's legal bases are: performance of the service or steps you request for core app and purchase functions; consent for consent-dependent advertising/privacy choices; legitimate interests for security, fraud prevention, service reliability and enforcement of reward/entitlement limits; and compliance with legal obligations where required. Subject to the GDPR, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, or object to processing. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing already lawfully carried out, and you may lodge a complaint with your competent supervisory authority. Where GDPR transfer rules apply, transfers must rely on an applicable adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs). Google and Apple/Google Play may process limited data outside the EEA under applicable law and their policies.
